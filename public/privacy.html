Privacy

This site is published for Bull Creek Fish Camp at 3861 County Rd 2006 W, Bunnell, FL 32110. It sets no cookies, runs no advertising trackers and no analytics scripts. Here is everything it does collect.

What's collected

The contact form, and a map tile

The contact form

When you send the form on the contact page, the name, phone, email and message you type are delivered by email to the camp's contact desk so the owners can answer you. Nothing else is stored on this site about you, and the information is used only to reply and to follow up about your note.

Email

If you email bullcreekfishcamp@gmail.com directly, your address and message are kept in the camp's mailbox so the conversation can be answered and referenced later.

The map on the contact and home pages

The embedded map is served by Google Maps. When the map loads in your browser, Google receives the standard request data of that request (including your IP address and which page embedded it). This site itself has no say in that and sets no cookies of its own.

No tracking

No cookies, no local storage, no pixels, no third-party scripts. The pages are plain files; your browser does all the work.

Your say

Want a copy of what you sent, or want it deleted from the desk's mailbox? Email bullcreekfishcamp@gmail.com and say which.

Anything else?

The contact desk answers questions about this site the same way it answers questions about the menu.
