Bull Creek Fish Camp

A family-run seafood restaurant coming back to the water on Bull Creek — breakfast, lunch and dinner, about 150 seats in a new elevated building, and a dock with boat slips so you can tie up and walk in.

The menu

Catfish, gator tail and the fish of the week

Seafood anchors the menu — freshwater and saltwater both: catfish, frog legs, gator tail, mahi, and a fish of the day or week that rotates with what the water gives up. Local beef is planned, and a lot of what reaches the table will be made in the kitchen, not off a truck.

Breakfast, lunch & dinner

Three meals a day, on the water — six days a week, closed on Tuesdays (planned).

The neighborhood

Where the nearest restaurant is 15 miles away

The camp serves the rural west side of Flagler County, where eating options are few — per Business Debut, Nov 2026. Local farmers and fishermen have stopped by during construction to say how eager they are to see the fish camp back. The building rises beside the water near Bull Creek Campground.
