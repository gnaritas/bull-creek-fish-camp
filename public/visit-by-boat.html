Dock & slips

Visit by boat on Bull Creek

The new building sits beside the water with a dock and boat slips — the owners built arrival-by-boat right into the camp, the way it worked at the original. Or drive up on County Rd 2006 W and eat with the boats.

The run in

Creek to lake, table at the end of it

Bull Creek runs out into Dead Lake here on the west side of Flagler County — regulars of the original camp spent their afternoons watching pleasure fishing boats come and go from the porch. per TripAdvisor reviews of the original camp The new camp keeps the water in the middle of it: guests can visit by boat and tie up at the camp's own dock. dock and slips per Business Debut, Nov 2026

By boat

New dock with boat slips at the front door — the creek run from Dead Lake ends at your table.

By land

Follow County Rd 2006 W to 3861 — the camp sits beside the water near Bull Creek Campground.
