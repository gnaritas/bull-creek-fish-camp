The camp, the flood, the family

Same creek. New building. The next family.

Bull Creek Fish Camp was a Bunnell institution for decades — then the water took the building, and a family that grew up eating here decided to build it back, higher up and ready for boats.

The timeline

How it went, and how it's coming back

  • The original camp

    Decades on the water

    Bull Creek Fish Camp fed Bunnell for a long time — the old listing carries 4.6 stars across 389 Google reviews, per Google Maps, pre-reopening listing and regulars remember the screened porch, the blackboard menu and the sunsets over the creek. per TripAdvisor reviews of the original camp

  • 2023

    The flood takes the building

    Back-to-back hurricanes led to severe flooding at the site. The old restaurant was torn down. per Business Debut, Nov 2026

  • 2025

    Construction starts on high ground

    Work began on a newly constructed building on elevated ground along the water, near Bull Creek Campground — about 150 seats, with a dock and boat slips. Local farmers and fishermen stopped by during construction to say how eager they were to see the camp back. per Business Debut, Nov 2026

  • January 2027

    The next family takes the dock

    Jessica Norton-Henry, her mother Pamela White and her brother Joshua White — "we're all local to Bunnell. We've all lived here our whole lives," Jessica, to Business Debut — are the new owners, targeting a January opening with the exact date still to be announced.

I think the nearest restaurant is 15 miles from us.

— Jessica Norton-Henry, to Business Debut, November 2026 — on serving a rural county with few places to eat

What carries over

The water, the menu and the handmade promise

What the new camp keeps from the old one is the setting and the spirit: meals on the water, seafood led by what's caught, and — in the owners' words — "a lot of stuff handmade, homemade." What's new is everything else: a newer building, on higher ground, built with a dock for the boats.
